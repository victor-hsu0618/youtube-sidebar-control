# Release Notes - 2026-11-18

## YouTube and YouTube Music Library

## Summary
Version 4.3 adds YouTube and YouTube Music playlist scanning, improves audio-only Music playback tracking, and makes Library and tab switching easier to use.

## Highlights
### Added
- Scan YouTube and YouTube Music playlists into Library, storing playlist details and links rather than copying the playlist's videos.

### Fixed
- YouTube Music audio-only playback now reports the active song title and playback progress.

### Improved
- Tab switch and relink prompts identify whether the target is YouTube or YouTube Music.
- Library remembers the last local group and scroll position; playlist scan controls are more compact.

### Changed
- Renamed the Library import action to Import and the group management gear to Manage.
